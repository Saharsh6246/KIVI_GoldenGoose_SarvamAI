# Product Positioning Statement

For people who do most of their work by speaking — into Slack, email, docs, code — Kivi already
writes what they say. Semantic memory makes Kivi the record of it: what you said, when, and where
it went, so Kivi can find it, reuse it, and answer questions about it when you ask. It does not
build a model of who you are. Every memory traces to a dictation you can replay, and deleting that
dictation deletes what it taught. Other assistants infer a person from their data. Kivi knows only
what that person chose to say out loud.
