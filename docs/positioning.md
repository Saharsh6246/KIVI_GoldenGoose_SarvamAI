# Product Positioning Statement
 
For people who do most of their work by speaking, into Slack, email, docs and
code, Kivi already writes what they say. Semantic memory makes Kivi the record
of it: what you said, when, and where it went, so Kivi can find it, reuse it and
answer questions about it when asked. It does not build a model of who you are.
Every memory traces to a dictation you can replay, and deleting it deletes what
it taught. Other assistants infer a person from their data. Kivi knows only what
you chose to say out loud.
